Configuration Inspector

options.showLauncher

Circular FAB renders a persistent 56×56px floating trigger at the viewport edge. Embedded mounts a direct 360×500px container inside host markup.

options.startMinimized

When true, the chat engine mounts with the discreet launcher bubble; clicking expands the chat. When false, the full chatbox renders open immediately.
